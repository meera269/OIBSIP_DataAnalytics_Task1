# OIBSIP_DataAnalytics_Task1
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to uncover purchasing patterns, sales trends, customer demographics, and business insights
## 📌 Objective
To perform a thorough Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to uncover purchasing patterns, monthly/quarterly sales trends, customer demographic characteristics, and provide actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack
- **Language:** Python
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook (Anaconda Distribution)
- **Libraries:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`

---

## 📊 Steps Performed
1. **Data Inspection:** Loaded dataset and checked shape, column data types, and null values.
2. **Descriptive Statistics:** Computed mean, median, mode, and standard deviation across numerical variables.
3. **Time Series Analysis:** Visualized monthly sales trends using line plots to identify seasonal peaks.
4. **Customer Demographics:** Analyzed age distribution and gender representation.
5. **Product Category Performance:** Identified top-selling product categories by total revenue using bar charts.
6. **Correlation Analysis:** Generated a correlation heatmap and cross-demographic spending heatmaps.

---

## 💡 Outcome & Business Recommendations
- **Inventory Planning:** Highlighted seasonal sales peaks to optimize inventory replenishment schedules before high-demand periods.
- **Target Marketing:** Identified key high-spending demographic age groups for customized ad targeting.
- **Product Bundling:** Recommended combining lower-selling category items with best-sellers to increase Average Order Value (AOV).
